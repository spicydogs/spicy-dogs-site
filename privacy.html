Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Spicy Dogs Behaviour handles personal information when you visit the website, contact me, subscribe to emails, purchase a resource or work with me.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Spicy Dogs Behaviour is operated by Vanessa and is based in Sri Lanka. For privacy questions or requests, contact vpetsservices@gmail.com.

2. Information I may collect

Depending on how you interact with Spicy Dogs Behaviour, I may collect:

  • your name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number;
  • information you provide through enquiry forms, questionnaires or discovery calls;
  • information about your dog, household, routines, behaviour concerns and goals that you choose to share;
  • messages, videos, photographs and other material you send during behaviour support;
  • newsletter subscription and communication preferences;
  • purchase and transaction information provided through third-party payment or commerce providers; and
  • basic technical information that may be generated when you use the website, such as IP address, browser or device information and website usage data where such tools are enabled.

I do not intentionally collect more personal information than is reasonably needed for the purpose for which you contacted me.

3. How I use personal information

I may use personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and arrange discovery calls;
  • assess whether my services are appropriate for you and your dog;
  • provide consultations, behaviour plans, follow-up support, video review and ongoing client support;
  • deliver digital products, guides and educational resources;
  • process and administer purchases and payments;
  • send newsletters or educational emails where you have subscribed;
  • maintain business records, improve services and protect the security of the website and business; and
  • meet legal, regulatory or administrative obligations.

Where consent is required, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

4. Newsletter and marketing emails

If you subscribe to the Spicy Dogs newsletter or request a free resource that includes email subscription, your details may be processed through Kit. You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in an email. Service-related messages necessary to deliver something you have purchased or requested are separate from marketing emails.

5. Third-party services

Spicy Dogs Behaviour uses third-party services to run the website and business. These may include Cloudflare for website hosting and delivery, GitHub for website code hosting, Kit for email subscriptions and digital commerce, Google Forms for questionnaires, WhatsApp/Meta for communications, and payment processors used to complete purchases.

When you use these services, your information may also be processed under that provider's own privacy policy and may be stored or processed outside Sri Lanka.

6. International data transfers

Some service providers used by Spicy Dogs Behaviour operate internationally. This means personal information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, I aim to use reputable providers and handle personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

7. Payments

Payments for products or services may be processed through third-party commerce or payment providers. I do not intentionally store full payment-card details on the Spicy Dogs website. Payment providers may collect information needed to complete and verify a transaction.

8. Cookies and website analytics

The website may use essential technologies required for site functionality and third-party embedded services. At present, Spicy Dogs does not rely on a separate advertising-tracking system on this website. If analytics, advertising cookies or similar tracking tools are introduced later, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated.

9. How long information is kept

I keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide ongoing or previous-client support, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or administrative requirements. Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or anonymised.

10. Security

I take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary email or messaging services.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022, as amended, provides rights and obligations concerning the processing of personal data. To make a privacy request, email vpetsservices@gmail.com. I may need to verify your identity before acting on certain requests.

12. Children's information

Spicy Dogs services and purchases are intended for adults. I do not knowingly seek personal information directly from children through this website. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, please contact me.

13. Links to other websites

The website may contain links to third-party websites or services. I am not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, so you should review their privacy information separately where relevant.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website, services, payment systems or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

15. Contact

For questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact vpetsservices@gmail.com.

Note: This policy is intended to describe the way Spicy Dogs Behaviour currently operates. It may be updated as new website features, analytics or payment systems are introduced.
